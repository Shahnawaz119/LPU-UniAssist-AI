# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Tuition Fees, Installments & Refund Policy

## Program Fee Structure (Per Semester, Standard Base Tuition)
- **B.Tech (Computer Science & Engineering)**: Standard base fee ₹1,40,000 per semester (before LPUNEST scholarship reduction).
- **B.Tech (Other Branches - ME, CE, ECE)**: Standard base fee ₹1,20,000 per semester.
- **BBA / BCA / B.Com**: Approximately ₹80,000 – ₹1,00,000 per semester.
- **MBA**: Approximately ₹1,90,000 – ₹2,00,000 per semester.
- **Examination Fee**: ₹2,500 – ₹4,000 per semester.
- *Note: Fee is reduced significantly based on your LPUNEST scholarship bracket (Bracket 1, Bracket 2, Bracket 3).*

## Payment Deadlines & Late Penalties
- **Odd Semester (Fall)**: Regular payment due date is usually July 15 – July 31.
- **Even Semester (Spring)**: Regular payment due date is usually December 15 – December 31.
- **Late Fee**: Late fee of ₹100/day applies up to 10 days; ₹200/day up to 20 days; after which UMS portal access and exam admit cards are automatically put on financial hold.

## Online Fee Payment & Installment Options
- **Online Payment Modes**: Log into **UMS (ums.lpu.in)** -> Navigate to **Fee Management** -> **Make Payment**. Accepts UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Debit/Credit Cards.
- **Installment Facilities**:
  - Students with genuine financial difficulties can apply for a **Fee Installment Plan** via the **Relationship Management System (RMS)** on UMS.
  - Choose: Category -> *Finance / Fee Installment Request*.
  - Requires approval from the Accounts Department. Permitted in 2 to 3 split installments per semester with zero or minimal processing charges.
  - Education loan facilitation is available directly on campus via major banks (SBI, PNB, Canara Bank, HDFC) at Block 30.

## Official Fee Refund Policy (As per UGC & LPU Guidelines)
Refunds for formal withdrawal of admission follow UGC mandate:
- **100% Refund**: Request submitted 15 days or more before the formally notified last date of admission (minus max ₹1,000 processing fee).
- **90% Refund**: Less than 15 days before the formally notified last date of admission.
- **80% Refund**: 15 days or less after the formally notified last date of admission.
- **50% Refund**: Between 16 to 30 days after the formally notified last date of admission.
- **0% Refund**: More than 30 days after the formally notified last date of admission.
- **How to apply for Refund Online**: Submit an online cancellation request through candidate portal (admission.lpu.in) or log an RMS ticket under *Accounts -> Admission Withdrawal*. Refund is credited to the bank account provided within 15–20 business days.
