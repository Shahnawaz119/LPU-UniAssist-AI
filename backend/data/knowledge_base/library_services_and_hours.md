# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Central Library & Knowledge Resources

## Central Library Location & Facilities
- **Main Location**: Central Library, Block 36 & Block 38 (Multi-storey state-of-the-art air-conditioned building).
- **Departmental Libraries**: Specialized reading wings located in Law (Block 27), Architecture (Block 35), and Management (Block 32).
- **Facilities**: Over 1.5 million print books, 30,000+ online journals, IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, Scopus, Springer, and EBSCO databases.
- **Digital Library & E-Resource Access**: Remote access to e-books and journals available through LPU OPAC / Knimbus portal using UMS login.

## Library Operating Hours
- **Regular Semester Days**:
  - Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM.
  - Sunday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
- **During Mid-Term (MTE) & End-Term (ETE) Examination Periods**:
  - **Open 24 Hours / 7 Days a week** with round-the-clock reading halls, Wi-Fi, and security.

## Book Borrowing & Return Policies
- **Undergraduate Students**: Up to 4 books for 14 days.
- **Postgraduate / Master's Students**: Up to 6 books for 21 days.
- **Ph.D. / Research Scholars**: Up to 10 books for 30 days.
- **Self-Checkout & Drop Boxes**: RFID automated kiosks for issuing and returning books without standing in queues.
- **Late Overdue Fine**: ₹2 per day per book for overdue general collection items.
- **Renewal Online**: Books can be renewed online once via the LPU OPAC portal if not reserved by another user.

## Discussion Rooms & Quiet Zones
- Soundproof discussion rooms available for group projects on the 2nd and 3rd floors.
- Silent reference reading halls strictly maintained on upper floors.
- High-speed photocopying and document scanning stations available on the Ground Floor.
