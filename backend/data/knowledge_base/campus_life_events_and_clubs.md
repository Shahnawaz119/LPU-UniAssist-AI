# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Campus Life, Student Clubs & Annual Events

## Division of Student Welfare (DSW) — Block 13
- **Location**: Block 13 (Central Hub for Student Activities, Clubs, Culture, Sports & Community Service).
- **Functions**: Manages student organizations, student elections/representatives, cultural and community initiatives, NCC (Army, Air Force, Navy wings), and NSS units.
- **Contact**: dsw@lpu.co.in | Phone: 01824-517000 (ext. DSW).

## Student Organizations & Technical Clubs
- **Clubs & Societies**: Over 150+ active student-driven organizations registered under DSW across technical, cultural, social, and literary domains:
  - Technical: Google Developer Student Club (GDSC), Coding Ninjas Club, Robotics & Drone Club, AI & Data Science Society.
  - Cultural & Creative: Music Society, Dance Club (Western & Folk / Bhangra), Dramatics & Street Play Society, Photography Club.
  - Literary & Debating: LPU Debating Society, Literary Cell, Model United Nations (LPU MUN).
- **How to Join a Club**: During the annual "Club Induction Fair" at Baldev Raj Mittal Unipolis, or register online on UMS -> Student Organizations -> Join Club.

## Mega Annual Festivals & Cultural Events
- **Youth Vibe**: LPU's flagship National Cultural and Technical Mega Fest. Hosts over 10,000+ outside participants featuring battle of bands, dance wars, fashion shows, hackathons, and star night celebrity concerts.
- **One India**: Celebrates Indian cultural diversity where thousands of students represent distinct Indian states with traditional food stalls, handlooms, and cultural parades.
- **One World**: Highlights the international student diaspora at LPU. Students from 50+ countries showcase international heritage, dances, and cuisine.
- **Spectra**: Annual inter-school cultural competition across singing, dancing, fine arts, and literary events.

## Sports & Indoor Stadium (Shanti Devi Mittal Indoor Stadium)
- **Facilities**: Olympic-sized indoor heated swimming pool, 12 badminton courts, squash courts, gymnastics arena, table tennis halls, and shooting range.
- **Outdoor Grounds**: Full-sized cricket stadium, synthetic turf football fields, volleyball, basketball, and tennis courts with floodlights.
- **Gymnasiums**: Fully equipped commercial-grade fitness gyms in BH-1, BH-4, BH-7, GH-1, and the Central Indoor Stadium.
