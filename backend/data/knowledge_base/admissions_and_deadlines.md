# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Admissions & LPUNEST

## Overview & Admission Process
- **Official Admission Portal**: [https://www.lpu.in](https://www.lpu.in) or [https://admission.lpu.in](https://admission.lpu.in)
- **Entrance & Scholarship Exam**: LPUNEST (National Entrance and Scholarship Test). It acts as both an eligibility entrance examination and the basis for merit-based scholarships.
- **Application Process**:
  1. Register on the official LPU admission portal (admission.lpu.in).
  2. Purchase LPUNEST Kit / Application Form online (₹1,000 for boys, ₹500 for girls).
  3. Book an exam slot (Test Center Based or Online Remotely Proctored Test from Home).
  4. Appear for LPUNEST, check results on the candidate dashboard, and complete seat allotment and initial payment.

## Academic Eligibility Requirements
- **B.Tech (CSE / Core Engineering)**: Minimum 60% aggregate marks in 10+2 (with Physics, Mathematics, and English) + valid score in LPUNEST (or JEE Main).
- **BBA / B.Com / BCA**: Minimum 50% to 55% aggregate in 10+2 with English.
- **MBA**: Minimum 55% in Bachelor's degree + LPUNEST / CAT / MAT / XAT / CMAT + Video Essay / Interview.
- **Postgraduate / Master's Programs**: Minimum 50% - 55% marks in graduation from a recognized university.
- **Relaxation**: 5% relaxation in eligibility marks for candidates from North-East states, Sikkim, Defense personnel wards, and Kashmiri migrants.

## Important Admission Dates & Phases
- **Phase 1 (Early Bird / Highest Scholarship)**: Applications open in January; Exam slots held in February – April.
- **Phase 2 & Subsequent Phases**: Held between May and July depending on seat availability.
- **LPUNEST Permitted Attempts**: Candidates are allowed up to a maximum of **2 attempts** for the LPUNEST exam during an academic session to improve their score and scholarship bracket.
- **Document Submission Deadline**: Freshmen must upload certified 10th/12th marksheets, transfer certificate, and migration certificate on UMS within 30 days of registration.
- **Physical Reporting & Verification Documents**: For on-campus physical reporting and document verification, students must bring original and self-attested photocopies of:
  - 10th marksheet / certificate (proof of date of birth)
  - 12th marksheet and passing certificate
  - Graduation degree and all semester marksheets (for Master's/PG programs)
  - Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate
  - Character Certificate from the last attended institution
  - Category / Community certificate (if applying under SC/ST/OBC/PwD quota)
  - Recent passport-size photographs and LPUNEST allotment letter.

## International Admissions & Visas
- Dedicated International Admissions Cell located in Block 30 (Division of Admissions).
- English proficiency requirement: Medium of instruction certificate in English or IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL score.
