# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Online Portals & UMS Digital Services

## University Management System (UMS) Online Features
- **Official URL**: [https://ums.lpu.in](https://ums.lpu.in)
- **Login Credentials**: Student Registration Number (Username) and Central Password.
- **Key Services Available Online**:
  - **Daily Attendance Check**: UMS -> Academic Services -> Attendance View. Updates in real-time after every class.
  - **Online Fee Payment & Receipts**: UMS -> Fee Management -> Make Payment. Download official fee receipts instantly.
  - **Datesheets & Hall Tickets / Admit Cards**: UMS -> Examination -> View Datesheet / Download Admit Card (available 7 days prior to MTE/ETE exams).
  - **Results & Grade Cards**: UMS -> Examination -> Grade Card / Result Summary. View SGPA, CGPA, and course grades online.
  - **Timetable & Faculty Directory**: UMS -> Academic Services -> View Time Table. Displays room numbers, lecture blocks, and faculty details.
  - **Hostel Gate Pass & Leave**: UMS / LPU Touch -> Hostel -> Apply Leave. Enables digital QR gate passes for exiting campus.

## Relationship Management System (RMS) — 24/7 Online Grievance & Query Resolution
- **Accessing RMS Online**: Log into UMS -> Click **RMS** in the top navigation or left sidebar.
- **How to Log an Online Ticket**:
  1. Select Problem Category (e.g., Accounts, Examination, Academic, Hostel, NetReg Wi-Fi).
  2. Select Sub-Category and enter your specific question or issue.
  3. Attach supporting screenshots or documents if required.
  4. Submit ticket to receive a unique RMS Tracking Number.
- **Tracking & Resolution**: Track resolution status online on UMS. Most tickets are addressed within 24 to 48 hours by designated university coordinators.

## LPU Online Learning (LMS) & Assignments
- **Learning Management System (LMS)**: [https://lms.lpu.in](https://lms.lpu.in)
- Access lecture slides, syllabus, reference reading materials, and previous year question papers.
- **Online Assignment & CA Submissions**: Submit PDF project reports and lab files online through LMS before the stated deadline.
- **Online Quizzes**: Periodic Continuous Assessment (CA) MCQ tests are conducted remotely or in campus computer labs via LMS.

## LPU Touch & LPU Live Mobile Apps
- **LPU Touch**: The all-in-one official mobile app for Android (Google Play Store) and iOS (Apple App Store).
  - **How to Access & Log in**: Download the LPU Touch app from your mobile app store, launch the application, and log in with your Student Registration Number and central UMS password.
  - **Services Available**: Live push notifications, real-time attendance alerts, datesheet, digital student ID card, and online hostel gate pass.
- **LPU Live**: Video conferencing and virtual classroom application used for online classes, remedial sessions, and placement webinars.
