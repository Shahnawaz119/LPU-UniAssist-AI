# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Hostels, Mess, Uni-Mall & Residence Life

## Hostel Accommodation (BH & GH Complexes)
- **Boys Hostels**: BH-1, BH-2, BH-3, BH-4, BH-5, BH-6, BH-7, BH-8.
- **Girls Hostels**: GH-1, GH-2, GH-3, GH-4, GH-5, GH-6 (secured with biometric entry and CCTV surveillance).
- **Room Types**:
  - 1-Seater, 2-Seater, 3-Seater, 4-Seater, and Dormitory rooms.
  - Available in both **Air-Conditioned (AC)** and **Air-Cooled** options.
  - Attached bathroom or communal washroom configurations.
- **Hostel Fee Range**: Approximately ₹70,000 to ₹1,80,000 per academic year depending on room occupancy (single/double/triple/dorm) and AC facilities.

## Mess & Dining Facilities
- **Mess Options**:
  - **Standard Mess**: North Indian and South Indian balanced meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, Dinner).
  - **Special Mess**: Includes dessert variety, continental options, and seasonal fruit servings.
  - **Ala Carte & Food Courts**: Fast food outlets, Subway, Dominos, Cafe Coffee Day, and Indian regional food corners located inside Uni-Mall and across academic blocks.
- **Mess timings**: Breakfast (7:30 AM – 9:30 AM), Lunch (12:00 PM – 2:30 PM), Evening Tea/Snacks (5:00 PM – 6:00 PM), Dinner (7:30 PM – 9:45 PM).

## Gate Pass, Night Out & Curfew Rules
- **Evening In-Time / Curfew**:
  - Girls Hostels: 7:30 PM (summer) / 7:00 PM (winter).
  - Boys Hostels: 9:00 PM / 9:30 PM.
- **Online Night Out & Leave Pass (Via UMS / LPU Touch App)**:
  - Step 1: Open UMS / LPU Touch -> Hostel -> Apply Leave / Gate Pass.
  - Step 2: Enter destination, departure time, and return date.
  - Step 3: Automated SMS / Call verification is sent to the registered parent's mobile number.
  - Step 4: Once parent approves via IVR / SMS, Warden approves on UMS, generating a digital QR code gate pass shown at the main turnstile gates.

## Campus Facilities: Uni-Mall & Amenities
- **Uni-Mall**: Multi-storey shopping complex inside campus containing supermarkets, departmental stores, clothing shops, electronics, salons, courier services, and bank ATMs.
- **Laundry Services**: Automated laundromats with 60 free washes per academic year included with hostel booking.
- **Gym & Sports**: Indoor sports complex (Shanti Devi Mittal Indoor Stadium) with Olympic-size swimming pool, badminton, squash, and basketball courts.

## Hostel Rules & Prohibited Items
- **Electrical Appliances Policy**: High-wattage electrical appliances such as electric room heaters, immersion water rods, induction cooktops, and electric kettles are strictly prohibited in hostel rooms for fire safety reasons. Unauthorized use leads to confiscation of the appliance and disciplinary fines.
- **Quiet Hours & Discipline**: Quiet hours are strictly observed from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM across all boys and girls hostel residential blocks.
