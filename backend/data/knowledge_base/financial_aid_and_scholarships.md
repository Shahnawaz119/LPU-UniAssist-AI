# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Scholarships & Financial Aid

## LPUNEST-Based Scholarships (Main Scheme)
LPU provides scholarship brackets directly linked to LPUNEST percentile / marks (or qualifying exam marks):
- **Bracket 1 (Top 10% / 95%+ in qualifying exam)**: Up to 50% – 60% concession on program tuition fee per semester.
- **Bracket 2 (Next 10% / 90%–94.9% in qualifying exam)**: Up to 40% – 45% concession on program tuition fee per semester.
- **Bracket 3 (Next 10% / 80%–89.9% in qualifying exam)**: Up to 30% – 35% concession on program tuition fee per semester.
- *Note: The earlier you appear in LPUNEST (e.g. Phase 1), the higher the scholarship quantum.*

## Other Merit & Special Scholarships
- **National & International Sports Honors**: Full fee waiver, free accommodation, and sports coaching for international, national, and state medal winners.
- **Wards of Defense Personnel & CAPF**: Special tuition fee concession (5% to 15%) for wards of army, navy, air force, and paramilitary forces.
- **Persons with Disabilities (PwD)**: Up to 50% scholarship for students with benchmark physical disabilities.
- **Orphan & Single Parent Concessions**: Need-based tuition assistance for students who have lost parents.
- **Sibling Concession**: 10% tuition concession for the younger sibling if two or more siblings study simultaneously at LPU.

## Criteria to Maintain / Renew Scholarship
To continue receiving the scholarship in subsequent semesters:
- Must maintain a minimum cumulative CGPA of **6.5 to 7.5** (depending on scholarship tier).
- Must have **no backlogs / no 'E' or 'F' grades** in any subject.
- Must maintain minimum **75% overall attendance**.
- Must have clean disciplinary record with no disciplinary warnings from the Proctorial Board or DSW.

## Government Scholarships & Verification
- State Post-Matric Scholarships (SC/ST/OBC/Minority) and National Scholarship Portal (NSP) schemes are processed via the Scholarship Cell at Block 30.
- Submit bonafide and fee structure certificates online through UMS -> Administrative Services.
