# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Uni-Hospital & Student Health Services

## Uni-Hospital (On-Campus Medical Center)
- **Location**: Uni-Hospital Building (Opposite Boys Hostel area / near Block 24).
- **Facilities**: Fully functional multi-bed hospital operating **24 Hours / 7 Days a week**.
- **Services Provided**:
  - Emergency triage, general OPD physicians, dental clinic, ENT specialists, and eye clinic.
  - In-house 24/7 diagnostic laboratory (blood tests, pathology, X-Ray, ECG).
  - 24/7 dedicated fleet of campus ambulances equipped with life-support systems for rapid transit to tertiary hospitals in Jalandhar or Ludhiana.
  - Fully stocked 24-hour medical pharmacy for prescription drugs.

## Medical Leave & Attendance Attestation
- If a student falls sick on campus, they must consult a Uni-Hospital medical officer.
- **Attestation Rule**: Outstation medical certificates must be produced before the Chief Medical Officer at Uni-Hospital within **3 working days** of returning to campus for formal attestation.
- Once stamped by Uni-Hospital, submit medical slip to the Dean/HOD office for attendance condonation consideration.

## Student Medical Insurance
- All admitted LPU students are covered under a group medical health insurance policy included in student welfare charges.
- Covers accidental hospitalization and treatment expenses up to designated policy limits.

## Counseling & Mental Well-being (Happiness Cell / DSW)
- **Location**: Division of Student Welfare (DSW), Block 13.
- Professional psychologists and counselors available for 1-on-1 confidential sessions addressing academic stress, anxiety, homesickness, and career guidance.
- Free appointments can be scheduled via UMS -> Student Welfare -> Counseling Appointment.
- **24/7 Emergency Helpline**: Campus Security & Medical Control Room: 01824-517100 / ext. 100.
