# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Course Registration, Attendance & Examination Policies

## UMS Course Registration & Electives
- **Portal**: [https://ums.lpu.in](https://ums.lpu.in) -> Academic Services -> Course Registration.
- **Electives / Minors / Specializations**: Students select Department Electives, Open Minor Courses, and Pathway Choices (Corporate Jobs, Higher Studies, Government Jobs, Entrepreneurship) at the start of each academic year through UMS.
- **Credit Requirements**: Standard semester credit load is 20 – 26 credits depending on the program curriculum.

## Mandatory 75% Attendance Policy (LPU Rule)
- **Minimum Required Attendance**: **75% mandatory attendance** in every individual course (theory and practical) to be eligible to sit in the End Term Examinations (ETE).
- **Attendance Condonation (Medical / Exceptional)**: Up to 10% condonation (allowing 65% minimum) can be granted strictly on valid medical grounds (hospitalization slip attested by Uni-Hospital doctor within 3 days of discharge) or approved Duty Leave (DL).
- **Duty Leave (DL)**: Granted for participating in university events, sports, hackathons, or cultural fests through the Division of Student Welfare (DSW). Apply on UMS within 48 hours.
- **Attendance Penalty / Detention**: Students with attendance below 75% (and not condoned) are marked **"Debarred / Detained" (DA)** and must clear the subject through summer term re-registration or reappear examinations.

## Examination Structure: CA, MTE, and ETE
- **Continuous Assessment (CA)**: Carries 25% – 30% weightage. Best 2 out of 3 CA tests or evaluations are counted towards the final score. Includes online quizzes on LMS, class assignments, presentations, and lab evaluations.
- **Mid-Term Examination (MTE)**: Conducted around the 7th–8th week of the semester.
- **End-Term Examination (ETE)**: Comprehensive semester exams conducted at the end of the term.
- **Prohibited Items in Exam Hall**: Mobile phones, digital/smart watches, programmable calculators, study notes, Bluetooth earphones, and unauthorized electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside examination halls. Standard non-programmable scientific calculators are permitted only where explicitly authorized on the question paper.
- **Grading System**: 10-point scale CGPA/SGPA (O: 10, A+: 9, A: 8, B+: 7, B: 6, C: 5, D: 4, E/F: Fail). Minimum passing grade is 'D' (typically 40% aggregate in the course).

## Re-appear / Reappear Examinations, Backlogs & Re-evaluation
- **Process to Apply for Reappear Examinations**: Students with backlogs can apply for Reappear examinations online through UMS under Examination -> Re-appear Registration / Backlog Examination Form.
- **Reappear Fee Per Course**: Standard Reappear examination fee is ₹500 to ₹1,000 per course / subject.
- **Scrutiny / Re-evaluation**: Students dissatisfied with their marks can apply for Answer Sheet Viewing or Re-evaluation on UMS within 7 days of result declaration.
