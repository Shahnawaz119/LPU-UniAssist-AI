# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — Placements, Training & Internships

## Division of Career Services (DCS) / Placement Cell
- **Location**: Division of Career Services, Block 30 & Block 32.
- **Role**: Coordinates all campus recruitment drives, summer internships, On-the-Job Training (OJT), and corporate networking with 1,000+ national and multinational companies.
- **Official Placement Portal**: Accessible via **UMS -> Placement Services -> Pehchan**.
- **Contact**: placements@lpu.co.in | Phone: 01824-517000.

## Placement Registration: The "Pehchan" Profile on UMS
- To become eligible for campus recruitment drives, every pre-final year and final-year student must register on **Pehchan (LPU Placement Portal on UMS)**.
- **Registration Process**:
  1. Open UMS -> Placement Services -> Pehchan Portal.
  2. Verify your academic credentials: 10th percentage, 12th percentage, diploma (if applicable), and all semester SGPA/CGPA.
  3. Upload your updated standard LPU format resume (verified by mentor).
  4. Complete biometric and mobile number verification.
  5. Agree to the LPU Placement Code of Conduct.

## Eligibility Criteria for Placement Drives
- **Minimum Academic CGPA**:
  - Super Dream Companies (₹15 LPA to ₹50+ LPA): 7.0 – 8.5+ CGPA with zero active backlogs.
  - Dream Companies (₹8 LPA to ₹15 LPA): 6.5 – 7.5 CGPA with zero active backlogs.
  - Core / Mass Recruiters (₹4.5 LPA to ₹7 LPA): 6.0 – 6.5 CGPA.
- **Backlog Policy**: Most premium companies require **zero active standing backlogs** at the time of the drive. Some companies additionally mandate no historical backlogs.
- **Mandatory Attendance in Training**: Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in Centre for Professional Enhancement (CPE) classes and technical mock test sessions.

## LPU Placement Policy: Categories & "One Student One Job" Rule
LPU follows a structured placement hierarchy to give every student fair opportunity while encouraging higher packages:
- **Tier 1 (Core / Standard Offer)**: Offers up to ₹7 LPA (e.g. TCS, Cognizant, Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini, DXC).
  - *Upgrade Rule*: A student placed in a Tier 1 company is eligible to sit for Dream and Super Dream companies.
- **Tier 2 (Dream Offer)**: Offers between ₹7.5 LPA and ₹14.9 LPA (e.g. Cisco, Oracle, Bosch, Optum, Virtusa, Tech Mahindra, Hitachi).
  - *Upgrade Rule*: A student with a Dream offer can only upgrade to a Super Dream company.
- **Tier 3 (Super Dream Offer)**: Offers ₹15 LPA and above (e.g. Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Palo Alto Networks, Apple, Adobe, Morgan Stanley).
  - *Final Offer Rule*: Once a student secures a Super Dream offer, their placement registration is closed to allow remaining peers to compete for upcoming drives.
- **Drive Discipline Penalty**: If a student registers for a company drive, takes the test, and skips the interview without prior written medical approval, they are debarred from the subsequent 3 to 5 campus placement drives.

## Centre for Professional Enhancement (CPE) — Placement Training
- **Curriculum Integration**: CPE courses are integrated into regular timetables starting from the 2nd/3rd year.
- **Modules Covered**:
  - Quantitative Aptitude and Logical Reasoning.
  - Verbal Ability, Soft Skills, Business Communication, and Corporate Etiquette.
  - Technical Mock Interviews, Coding Sprints (Data Structures & Algorithms in C++, Java, Python), and System Design.
  - Domain certifications: AWS Cloud, Salesforce, Microsoft Azure, and Oracle certifications sponsored on campus.

## Internships & On-Job-Training (OJT)
- **Final Year 6-Month / 1-Year Internships**: Final-year students who secure full-time internships with stipends can convert their semester into On-Job-Training (OJT).
- **No Objection Certificate (NOC)**: Apply online via UMS -> Placement -> Internship / OJT NOC Request.
- Requires offer letter verification by DCS and approval by the Head of Department (HOD) and Dean.
- Academic credits for the final semester are awarded through periodic industry mentor reviews and project report evaluations submitted on LMS.
