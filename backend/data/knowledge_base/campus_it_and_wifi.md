# Lovely Professional University (LPU) — IT Services, Wi-Fi & Technical Support

## Campus Wi-Fi Connectivity
- **Wi-Fi SSID**: `LPU-Student-WiFi` / `LPU-Campus-WiFi`.
- **Login Credentials**:
  - **Username**: Your Student Registration Number (e.g., `12345678` or `12400123`).
  - **Password**: Your University Central UMS Password.
- **Device MAC Registration (NetReg)**:
  - Each student can register up to 2 devices (e.g. 1 Laptop, 1 Smartphone).
  - Open browser on connected Wi-Fi -> go to `netreg.lpu.in` or access via UMS -> Wi-Fi Registration.
  - Enter device MAC address and submit.

## University Management System (UMS) & Mobile Apps
- **Web Portal**: [https://ums.lpu.in](https://ums.lpu.in) (Single sign-on for attendance, grades, fee payment, timetable, hostel leave, and exam admit cards).
- **LPU Touch Mobile App**: Available for download on Google Play Store (Android) and Apple App Store (iOS).
  - **How to Access & Log in**: Download the app from your app store, open it, and log in using your University Student Registration Number as username and your central UMS password.
  - **Features**: Provides live notifications, class schedules, daily attendance tracking, exam admit cards, hostel leave application, and digital student ID card.
- **LPU Live App**: Platform for online classes, guest lectures, webinars, and virtual mentor meetings.

## Relationship Management System (RMS) — Online Helpdesk & Grievances
- **RMS is LPU's official ticket resolution portal** accessible via UMS -> Relationship Management System (RMS).
- Students can log complaints, service requests, or administrative inquiries across categories:
  - Academics (Timetable, Teacher queries, Attendance correction)
  - Examination (Result discrepancy, Hall ticket issue)
  - Finance / Accounts (Fee receipt, refund, installment)
  - Hostel & Mess (Maintenance, food feedback, laundry, Wi-Fi)
  - IT Services (Password reset, portal bug, NetReg)
- **Turnaround Time (TAT)**: Most RMS tickets are resolved by dedicated coordinators within 24 to 48 hours.

## Student Software Licenses
- **Microsoft 365 (Office ProPlus)**: Free official subscription for Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, and Teams using `@lpu.in` email ID.
- **OneDrive Cloud Storage**: 1 TB cloud storage provided for academic projects and notes.
- **MATLAB, AWS Academy, Oracle Academy**: Specialized lab software accessible through university network licenses.
